# Brewmark feedback desk

## What this is
Brewmark Coffee sells coffee beans and grinders online in India, mostly by subscription. Every week, customer messages arrive from email, WhatsApp, Instagram, Twitter and app reviews. This folder turns them into triage, reply drafts and a one-page board.

## Folder map
- inputs/ - the week's messages as CSV (id, date, channel, message). Never edit these.
- work/ - triage.csv and replies.md, rewritten on every run
- board.html - the one-page board for the team

## Rules
1. Never change a customer's words. Quote them exactly.
2. Never promise a refund, a date or compensation in a reply. Say who will act and by when we'll update them.
3. Agents replace their own files in work/ on every run.
4. Plain, warm words. No corporate phrases.
